Studio Supply is a fictional stationery shop and a shopping-cart learning project by Dash. It brings together a few colourful desk essentials in a simple, thoughtfully designed storefront.

Explore the collection, add a favourite to your bag, adjust quantities and complete a demo order. Your bag is saved in this browser when storage is available. There are no real purchases, payments or deliveries.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with original SVG product illustrations. No framework, account or installation needed.
